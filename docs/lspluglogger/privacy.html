LSPLUGLogger privacy policy

Data handled on your iPad

LSPLUGLogger stores EEG and motion samples, recording times, device identifiers, participant identifiers or nicknames, researcher and project identifiers, protocol labels, calibration results and signal annotations. Local participant settings can also contain the date of birth, sex and handedness you enter. The app does not need the previous PLUG Logger cloud service.

If camera recording is enabled, video is stored locally alongside the EEG recording. Microphone audio is not recorded. Local network triggers can include a label, trial number, text, reception time and sender address; these are stored with the recording.

Google Drive access and transfers

Google Drive is optional. Google handles sign-in and asks you to authorize access. The app uses the drive.file permission to check your chosen destination folder and create or update this app's recording files there. File identifiers, parent folders, file sizes and checksums are used to verify uploads and avoid duplicates.

Closed CSV files and their matching JSON session information are sent directly from your iPad to Google Drive over HTTPS. This includes interrupted and calibration recordings. Videos are not automatically uploaded to Drive. If you use a share button, the files you select are passed to the sharing destination you choose.

The destination folder's Drive sharing settings determine who can access uploaded data. The developer does not receive recordings through a separate collection server.

Storage and retention

Google authorization tokens are stored in the iPad Keychain. They are not written to recording files, configuration JSON or application logs. Folder identifiers and upload tracking information are stored locally.

Local files remain on your iPad until you delete them, for example through the Files app. Uploaded copies remain in Drive until you delete them there. Deleting one copy does not delete the other.

Your controls

You can disable video and LAN triggers in the app settings. You can disconnect Google Drive to remove local Google credentials and stop uploads, or revoke access in your Google Account settings. Disconnecting does not delete recordings.

Other uses

The app has no advertising, analytics service or AI-training integration. Google user data is used only for the authorized recording uploads described here. The app's use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
