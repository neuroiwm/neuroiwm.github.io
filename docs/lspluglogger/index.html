LSPLUGLogger

LSPLUGLogger is an iPad app for research EEG recording with supported LS PLUG hardware. It stores EEG recordings as CSV files and session information as JSON files. Participant, researcher, project and protocol settings are managed on the iPad.

The app includes live waveform and spectrum displays, resting EEG calibration and research signal annotations. These displays and annotations do not establish a medical diagnosis.

Local video and LAN triggers

When enabled, the iPad camera records a local video alongside EEG. The app does not record microphone audio. An optional UDP receiver accepts recording labels from another computer on the same local network.

Optional Google Drive uploads

You can connect a Google account and authorize a destination folder. Once configured, closed CSV recordings and matching JSON session information are uploaded automatically. This includes interrupted recordings and calibration recordings. Video files remain on the iPad unless you choose to share them yourself.

The app requests the drive.file permission for the Drive files and folder you use with the app. It does not request access to your entire Drive, email or contacts. There is no separate developer-operated upload server.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before use. Installation and research procedures are arranged by your research team.
