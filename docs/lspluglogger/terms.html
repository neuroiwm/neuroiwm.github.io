LSPLUGLogger terms of use

LSPLUGLogger is provided for research EEG recording with supported hardware. Use the recording and calibration procedures arranged by your research team. Signal annotations are research outputs and do not establish a medical diagnosis.

Record participants or video only when you are authorized to do so and have obtained the consent required by your study. Connect Google Drive only if you are authorized to upload the recordings and session information to the selected folder. You retain your rights in your recordings; the app does not transfer ownership to its developer.

Drive use is optional and remains subject to Google's service terms and your institution's rules. Uploads may be delayed by connectivity, permissions or app suspension. Keep local files until you have confirmed the copies you need.

You can stop uploads by disconnecting Google Drive in the app. Contact the address below about installation or use.
