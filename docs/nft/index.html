NFT iPad

NFT iPad is a research app for recording EEG and running neurofeedback sessions on iPad. It connects to a supported EEG device, saves CSV recordings and JSON session information on the iPad, and provides configurable neurofeedback.

Optional Google Drive uploads

You can connect a Google account and authorize a specific destination folder. The app then uploads completed CSV recordings and their JSON session information to that folder. Local recordings remain on the iPad.

The app requests access only to the Drive files and folder you use with this app. It does not request access to your entire Drive, email, or contacts.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before connecting Google Drive. Installation is arranged by the developer.
