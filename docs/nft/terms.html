NFT iPad terms of use

NFT iPad is provided for research EEG recording and neurofeedback sessions. Use it with the supported hardware and the recording procedures arranged by your research team.

Connect a Google account only if you are authorized to upload the recordings and session information to the selected folder. You retain your rights in your recordings. This app does not transfer ownership of your recordings to its developer.

Google Drive use is optional and remains subject to Google's service terms and your institution's rules for the data you record. Uploads can be delayed by connectivity, account permissions or app suspension; keep local recordings until you have confirmed the copies you need.

You can stop using Google Drive uploads by disconnecting in the app. Questions about installation or use can be sent to the contact below.
