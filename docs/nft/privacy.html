NFT iPad privacy policy

Data the app handles

NFT iPad stores EEG recordings and session information locally on your iPad. Depending on your configuration, recordings can include EEG and motion samples, timestamps, device identifiers, participant identifiers or nicknames, protocol settings, channel labels, feedback bands and scores.

Google Drive access and use

Google Drive uploads are optional. Google handles sign-in and asks you to grant access. The app uses the drive.file permission to check the destination folder you select and to create or update this app's recordings there. It checks file identifiers, parent folders, file sizes and checksums to confirm uploads and avoid duplicates.

Only completed CSV recordings and their matching JSON session information are uploaded. Files are sent directly from your iPad to Google Drive over HTTPS. There is no separate developer-operated upload server. The destination folder's Google Drive permissions determine who can access uploaded recordings.

Storage and retention

Google authorization tokens are stored in the iPad's Keychain and refreshed to support subsequent uploads. Folder identifiers and upload tracking information are stored locally. Tokens are not written to recording files, configuration JSON or application logs.

Recordings remain on the iPad until you delete them. Uploaded copies remain in Google Drive until you delete them there. Deleting a local copy does not delete its Drive copy, and deleting a Drive copy does not delete the local recording.

Your controls

You can disconnect Google Drive in NFT. This removes locally stored Google credentials and stops uploads; it does not delete recordings. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings. Google Drive uploads can be used only when the selected account can write to the destination folder.

Sharing and other uses

The app sends Google user data only to the destination you authorize, as described above. The app has no advertising, analytics service or AI-training integration. The developer does not receive recordings through a separate collection service. The app's use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
